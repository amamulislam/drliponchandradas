Dr. Lipon Chandra Das

Dr. Das currently serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh. Prior to this role, he worked as a Lecturer at BGC Trust University Bangladesh and Premier University from January 2012 to June 2018.

He holds a B.Sc. (Honors) in Mathematics, an M.S., and an M.Phil. in Pure Mathematics from the University of Chittagong, awarded in 2010, 2012, and 2018, respectively. He completed his PhD at Shandong University, China (2019–2024), focusing on climate modelling and data-driven prediction.

His research interests encompass Climate Change in Bangladesh, AI, Data Analysis, Data Mining, Deep Learning, Applied Statistics, and Optimisation & Non-linear Programming. His academic journey reflects a deep commitment to both teaching and research.
